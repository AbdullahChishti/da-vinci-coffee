Create a minimal, modern, full-screen website for a specialty coffee brand called "da vinci".

Goal:
A calm, premium website with subtle cinematic video — not flashy, not busy.

Style:
- Warm neutral palette (#E8DCCB, #3A2F2A, wood tones)
- Elegant serif typography (Playfair Display)
- Clean layout, lots of whitespace
- Soft shadows, subtle textures
- Editorial / luxury aesthetic

IMPORTANT:
Use video only in key sections. Keep it subtle, slow, and muted.
No autoplay sound.

---

Structure:

1. Hero Section (VIDEO BACKGROUND)
- Full-screen video (looped, muted)
- Content of video:
  slow espresso extraction OR soft café interior light

- Overlay text (centered):

  da vinci
  art meets reason

- Slight dark overlay for readability
- Smooth fade-in on load

---

2. Concept Section (NO VIDEO)
- Static section
- Clean text:

  “A quiet space for coffee.
   Measured. Observed. Balanced.”

- Large spacing, minimal design

---

3. Coffee Section (SUBTLE VIDEO OR STATIC)
- Either:
  - short loop of coffee pouring OR
  - static minimal layout

- Keep video small or background-only
- Do NOT distract from content

---

4. Experience Section (VIDEO FEATURE)
- Background video:
  tray ritual (cup placement, water glass, slow motion)

- Overlay text:
  “Placed, not dropped.”

- Text aligned left or center

---

5. Loyalty Section (STATIC)
- Title: “The Study”
- Minimal text:
  “Complete 7 studies. Unlock a creation.”

---

6. Footer (STATIC)
- Simple layout
- No video

---

Video Rules:
- Slow motion only
- Warm color grading
- Muted, looped, seamless
- Use <video> tag with:
  autoplay, muted, loop, playsinline

---

Interactions:
- Smooth scroll
- Soft fade-in animations
- No aggressive motion
- No parallax overload

---

Technical:
- HTML, CSS, JavaScript (or React)
- Fully responsive
- Optimize video loading (low file size)
- Lazy load videos below the fold

---

Avoid:
- Multiple videos on screen at once
- Bright colors
- Fast cuts
- Overly commercial feel

---

Goal:
Make the website feel calm, immersive, and cinematic — like stepping into the café.
